# Camera setup

Start with a phone or existing camera on a secure, fixed mount behind the
baseline, centered on the court's long axis. The PRD recommends elevation of
3–5 m and a downward angle of approximately 15–25 degrees. Keep both players and
the four singles corners visible throughout the recording. Avoid fence/net
occlusion and camera movement.

Prefer 1080p at 60 fps or higher; 4K helps distant players but increases storage
and processing. Lock focus/exposure when the device allows it. A fast shutter
around 1/500 s helps avoid motion blur in sufficient lighting. Secure any elevated
mount so it cannot fall onto the court. Check power and free storage before a session.

Phone: easiest initial footage source, with a stable fence/tripod mount and a power
bank for longer sessions. Action camera: wide coverage, but avoid excessive
wide-angle distortion. IP camera: check that it records an exportable MP4/MOV
locally; live RTSP ingestion is not implemented.

Upload a short representative clip first. Optional calibration in the upload
dialog asks for far-left, far-right, near-right, near-left singles corners.
Recalibrate when the camera moves. Use the same setup across sessions for trends.
The exact camera choice remains open until real sample footage is available.
